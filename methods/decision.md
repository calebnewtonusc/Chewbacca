# Making a call

**Falsifier:** what would I learn that would flip this?

**Cheapest test:** can I find that out before committing, and what would it
cost?

## Questions that change the decision

- Is this reversible? Reversible decisions should be made fast and cheaply;
  the deliberation belongs to the ones that are not.
- What is the cost of being wrong in each direction? They are almost never
  symmetric and the asymmetry usually IS the answer.
- What am I optimising for, and did anyone say so out loud?
- Whose call is this actually? Some decisions are not mine to make and the
  respectful move is one clear question rather than a confident guess.
- What happens if I do nothing? The null option is real and is usually
  omitted from the comparison.
- Am I deciding, or building to avoid deciding?

## The trap specific to decisions

A decision presented with three balanced options is usually a decision being
avoided. Lean on one and say why.
