Promoted by `consolidate --promote` from the kit's own recorded
failures. Every rule names the episodes it came from, so the claim
can be checked against the commits rather than believed.

These are PROPOSALS. A rule nobody believes teaches people to skip
rules, so delete any that do not survive reading.
