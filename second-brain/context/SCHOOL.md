# School: the term, the courses, the standing

**Updated:** When a term starts, a grade posts, a deadline moves, or an absence happens.
**Update trigger:** Any of those, in the same turn they are mentioned.

The dates themselves do not live here. They live in the coursework ledger
(`~/coursework`), which is structured YAML that the `coursework` CLI reads.
This file holds the things a ledger cannot: what is actually going on this term,
what is at risk, and the decisions that are still open.
