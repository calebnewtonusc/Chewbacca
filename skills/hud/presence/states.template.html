Presence states

All seven states of the field, running the shader the overlay runs, with the numbers read out of Presence.field at build time. The question this page exists to answer is not whether any one of them looks good. It is whether you can tell which one is up without looking straight at it.

Peripheral vision

A blur standing in for acuity outside the fovea. It is a proxy and not a simulation: the honest test is to open this, look at your other monitor, and say out loud which tile just changed.

Which pairs collide

Every pair of states, by how far apart their two visible channels sit. A pair that is close on both is a pair you will not tell apart at the edge of your eye, whatever either one looks like on its own.
